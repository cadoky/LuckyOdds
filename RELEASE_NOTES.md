First public release of LuckyOdds (beta).

- Odds panel: your Rare / Epic / Legendary chances for spells, rings, trinkets and amulets at your current luck and level
- Minimap: Battleground Cache icons, with an edge arrow to the nearest one (hover for distance)
- Minimap: guardian lairs (asleep, fighting, countdown when empty, "due" when it's about to spawn)
- Commands: `/lucky`, `/lucky chests`, `/lucky lairs`

The odds are a rough guide, not the game's exact roll. If you use Chestlight, turn it off or you'll see the chests twice.

Install: drop the `LuckyOdds` folder into your `AddOns` folder.
